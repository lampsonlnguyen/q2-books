Books v0.9.0-beta for the Shanling Q2 (Q2 Pod card app). Everything merged on `main` since v0.8.0-beta. Built by GitHub Actions after the GCC/Clang test suites and the Q2 MIPS/QEMU checks.

> **Beta, not yet tested on a physical Q2.** All checks so far ran on host builds, ASan, a fake Q2 (real input socket and framebuffer) and Q2 MIPS/QEMU. Report problems with the Q2 Pod firmware version.

## Install
Extract the ZIP to the root of the Q2's SD card (it adds `.books/` and the card-app files). Do not flash it as firmware. Settings, positions, bookmarks and highlights from v0.8 are kept.

## New since v0.8.0-beta

**Network (v0.9)**
- HTTPS for OPDS/Kavita and KOReader progress sync, through Q2 Pod's libcurl. Certificate chain and hostname checks are mandatory; roots come from `.books/ca-bundle.pem` (Mozilla list, shipped). Same-origin redirects only, no HTTPS→HTTP downgrade. Plain HTTP still works for a trusted LAN.
- Transfers can be stopped, time out when stalled, and never leave half-downloaded EPUBs on the shelf.
- OPDS **More results** for catalogs that page their lists, and **Search this catalog** for catalogs with OpenSearch.

**Reading (v0.10)**
- Nine reading fonts at 10, 11 and 12 pt: Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Bitter, Lexend Deca, ChareInk, Literata, Merriweather, Inter, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next.
- Go to percent, auto page turn (10–60 s; for runs longer than 2 minutes turn on Q2 Pod's Smart Awake), focus reading (partial bold).
- Plain-text `.txt` books on the shelf.
- Footer shows page and book percentage.

**Dictionaries (v0.11)**
- StarDict dictionaries (`.ifo/.idx/.dict/.dict.dz/.syn`) in `.books/dictionaries/<name>/` or `Dictionaries/<name>/` on the card; `dictionary.tsv` still works. Define shows every match, paged with the wheel.

**Fixes**
- Long-press selected the word on the line below the one pressed.
- Chapter and bookmark lists: rows overlapped and the footer ran out of the panel. Book and catalog lists: title and author lines touched (now 4 taller rows).
- Wheel input under fast turning, resume after the app is killed, header and menu text clipping, entity-bomb and overlong-word layout stalls, and the release ZIP containing test files.

Details: `docs/v0.9.0-https-transport.md`, `docs/v0.10-v0.11-reading-dictionaries-opds.md`, `docs/v0.10.0-font-size-10-12.md`.

## Known limits
- Menus have no CJK glyphs (book text does). Dictionary matching ignores ASCII case only.
- Battery, suspend/resume, and memory on the real device are not measured yet.
- A server certificate with a CN but no subjectAltName may be accepted, depending on the Q2's libcurl.
