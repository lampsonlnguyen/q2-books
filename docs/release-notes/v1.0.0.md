Books 1.0.0 for the Shanling Q2: an ebook reader card app for Q2 Pod, built on the CrossPoint Reader engine (EPUB layout, fonts and caches) ported to the Q2's Linux. Formerly released as CrossPod; this is the first release under the name **Books**.

Built by GitHub Actions after the GCC/Clang test suites and the Q2 MIPS/QEMU checks.

## Install
Extract the ZIP to the root of the Q2's SD card: it adds `.books/` (program, fonts, certificates) and sample books in `Books/`. Do not flash it as firmware. In Q2 Pod the app is **Books** in the Library.

**Upgrading from CrossPod:** on its first start Books copies your reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, settings, recent books, `connected.conf` and `dictionary.tsv` from the old `.crosspod` folder, and moves `dictionaries/`. The old folder is then unused; delete it when you are satisfied.

## What it does
- **Reading:** EPUB (CrossPoint's engine: CSS, images, footnotes and links, multilingual and right-to-left text) and plain-text `.txt`. Nine fonts at 10/11/12 pt; line spacing, margins, alignment, hyphenation, book styles, night mode, focus reading. Go to percent, auto page turn (10–60 s; turn on Q2 Pod's Smart Awake for runs over 2 minutes), page and book percentage in the footer. Positions kept per book and on exit.
- **Library:** shelf with covers, recent books, sorting, folders, and search with the Q2's T9 keypad.
- **Notes and words:** bookmarks, highlights, text selection; StarDict dictionaries (`.idx/.dict/.dict.dz/.syn`) in `.books/dictionaries/` or `Dictionaries/` on the card, plus `dictionary.tsv`.
- **Online:** OPDS catalogs (Kavita and others) over HTTPS or LAN HTTP, with catalog search and paging, EPUB downloads, and manual KOReader-compatible progress sync. Set up in `.books/connected.conf` (see `connected.conf.example`).
- **About:** Reading settings → About.

## Known limits
- Not yet measured on a physical Q2: battery, suspend and resume, memory over long sessions. Report problems with your Q2 Pod firmware version.
- Menus have no CJK glyphs (book text does). Dictionary matching ignores ASCII case only.
- A server certificate with a CN and no subjectAltName may be accepted, depending on the Q2's libcurl.
