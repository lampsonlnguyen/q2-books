Books 1.1.1 for the Shanling Q2: an ebook reader card app for Q2 Pod, built on the CrossPoint Reader engine ported to the Q2's Linux. Built by GitHub Actions after the GCC/Clang test suites and the Q2 MIPS/QEMU checks.

## Install
Extract the ZIP to the root of the Q2's SD card: it adds only the app's folder, `.books/`. Put your `.epub` and `.txt` books in the card's `Books` folder. Do not flash it as firmware. Settings, positions, bookmarks and highlights from 1.0/1.1 are kept; upgrading from CrossPod copies them from `.crosspod` on first start.

## New since 1.1.0
- **Q2 Pod's look:** Books follows the system theme (Classic, Modern or Minimal, Light Mode too) that Q2 Pod hands every card app: Classic's white pages, glossy title strip and blue band with a chevron; Modern's dark pages and accent bar; Minimal's black pages and dot. The keypad, highlights and menus follow it; menus use smooth letters. Older Q2 Pod firmware keeps Books' own look.
- **Menus in Vietnamese:** Reading settings → Language (English / Tiếng Việt).
- **Your own fonts:** put `<Name>-12.cpfont` (and optionally `-10`, `-11`) in a `Fonts` folder at the card root; up to four appear in Reading settings → Font.
- **Export notes:** Reading settings → Export notes writes the book's highlights (with their words) and bookmarks to `Clippings/<title>.txt` on the card.
- **Dictionaries:** lookups find words whatever their case, including Vietnamese, Greek and Cyrillic capitals; a phrase with no entry is looked up by its first word; Reading settings → Dictionaries turns each one on or off; each defined word is logged to `.books/vocabulary.tsv`.

## Known limits
- Not yet measured on a physical Q2: battery, suspend and resume, memory over long sessions.
- Menus have no CJK letters (book text does). Layout spacing is Books' own, not Q2 Pod's 32 px rows; there is no status bar (clock, battery) in the app.
- Highlights made before 1.1.1 export without their words.
- A server certificate with a CN and no subjectAltName may be accepted, depending on the Q2's libcurl.
