Books 1.2.0 for the Shanling Q2: an ebook reader card app for Q2 Pod, built on the CrossPoint Reader engine ported to the Q2's Linux. Built by GitHub Actions after the GCC/Clang test suites and the Q2 MIPS/QEMU checks.

## Install
Extract the ZIP to the root of the Q2's SD card: it adds only the app's folder, `.books/`. Put your `.epub` and `.txt` books in the card's `Books` folder. Do not flash it as firmware. Settings, positions, bookmarks and highlights from 1.0 through 1.1.2 are kept.

## New since 1.1.2
- **Smoother text:** letters are anti-aliased. The fonts' grey edges were drawn as solid black before, which looked jagged on the Q2's screen; they now blend into the theme's colours. Pictures show their greys too. Works with every Q2 Pod theme and night mode.
- **Clock and battery while reading:** the bottom line shows the time, the progress bar, the page and percent, and the battery (with a bolt while charging). It updates when the minute or the battery changes.
- **Q2 Pod's menu layout:** Reading settings, Chapters, Bookmarks and Dictionaries use the system's 32 px rows, eight to a screen, with values on the right and a full-width selection, under a bar with the title, clock and battery. Tapping a row picks the row under your finger; a tap on the bar or footer does nothing.
- **More books open:** a chapter with a stray `&`, `&nbsp;` or `&copy;`, an unclosed or mismatched tag, an HTML `<br>`, or that is cut off now shows its text instead of a blank book.

## Known limits
- Not yet measured on a physical Q2: page-turn time with smooth text (each new page is drawn three times; pages you come back to are not), battery, suspend and resume, memory over long sessions.
- The clock is 24-hour only: Q2 Pod's 12/24-hour setting cannot be read by a card app.
- In a repaired chapter, an unclosed bold or italic carries on into the next paragraphs.
- Menus have no CJK or Thai letters, so Japanese, Korean, Thai and Chinese menus stay in English (book text in those languages shows).
- Highlights are not synced to the server.
- A server certificate with a CN and no subjectAltName may be accepted, depending on the Q2's libcurl.
