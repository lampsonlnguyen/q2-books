# Books v1.2.4 for Shanling Q2

- The current book's cover and text are vertically centered between the separators on the Books home screen.
- The current book title is slightly smaller so more of a long title fits on one line.

## Install

Extract `books-q2-v1.2.4.zip` to the root of your Q2's microSD card. It installs the `.books/` card app for Q2 Pod. Keep your EPUB and TXT files in the card's `Books/` folder.
