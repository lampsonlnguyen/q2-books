# Books v1.9.0 for Shanling Q2

- Books opens with a Q2 Pod menu that uses the firmware’s theme, colors, and status bar. Use it to resume reading or open Browse Files, Books Home, or Options.
- **Page warmth** in Reading settings lets you choose Cool, Default, Warm, or Warmer in light mode. Books remembers your choice.
- Books Home shows **Hold Centre to browse** when Continue Reading is highlighted and there are multiple recent books.

Extract the ZIP to the microSD root and keep your existing reading data. On older firmware that cannot open the native menu’s full-screen rows, copy `.books/q2app.fullscreen` over `.books/q2app` to launch Books Home directly.
