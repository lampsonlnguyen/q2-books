# M6: polish for the Q2 (v0.14)

Status: **first three items implemented on a branch; not released**. Checked
on the host (GCC, Clang, ASan) and on the fake Q2 framebuffer in all three
Q2 Pod themes and with no theme. Not yet measured on a physical Q2.

## Smooth text

**Before:** reading text was jagged. CrossPoint's fonts store four levels of
coverage per pixel (white, light grey, dark grey, black), but the black and
white pass paints every non-white level as solid ink. That suits e-ink; on the
Q2's LCD it gives stair-stepped, slightly heavy letters.

**Now:** after a page is drawn in black and white, the reader draws it twice
more in the engine's own grayscale modes (`GRAYSCALE_LSB`, `GRAYSCALE_MSB`),
which is what upstream CrossPoint does for e-ink anti-aliasing. The two planes
give each ink pixel its level back:

| Coverage | BW | MSB | LSB | Shown as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | paper | 0 | 0 | paper |
| light grey | ink | 1 | 0 | 104/255 of the way to ink |
| dark grey | ink | 1 | 1 | 184/255 of the way to ink |
| black | ink | 0 | 0 | ink |

The presenter blends each pixel between the paper and ink colours of its
theme zone, so it works with every theme and with night mode. Images use the
same planes, so their two greys are shown too instead of solid black.

Rules that keep it correct:

- A pixel takes its grey only while it still shows the rendered page. A
  menu, highlight, the progress bar, or anything else drawn over the page
  stays plain ink or paper (`Q2Display::computeLevels`).
- Night mode inverts the whole page; the levels flip with it.
- Clearing the screen drops the planes, so a menu never inherits a page's.
- The three-page cache keeps the planes, so turning back costs nothing.

**Cost:** two extra page renders the first time a page is drawn. On the host,
20 page turns took 0.42 s of CPU against 0.16 s without smooth text; peak memory
rose by 130 to 210 KB (planes, levels, cached planes). The level pass itself is
0.3 ms. Upstream runs the same two passes on the ESP32, which is slower than
the Q2's X2000, but **page-turn time on a Q2 is not measured yet**.
`BOOKS_SMOOTH_TEXT=0` in the environment turns it off.

## Status in the reading footer

The footer now reads `clock | progress bar | page and percent | battery`:

- **Clock:** local time from `localtime_r`, as Q2 Pod's bar reads it; the card
  app inherits Q2 Pod's `TZ`. 24-hour form (`18:14`): Q2 Pod's 12/24-hour
  setting lives in firmware memory only, so Books cannot follow it.
- **Battery:** the percent and an icon, from
  `/sys/class/power_supply/battery/capacity`, else the CW2015 fuel gauge
  (`/sys/devices/i2c-0/0-0062/cw2015_capacity`); a bolt while
  `/sys/class/power_supply/battery/status` is Charging or Full. Without a
  readable battery the icon is left out.
- **Refresh:** checked once a second while a page shows with nothing over it,
  and the page is presented again only when the minute or the battery
  changes. Reading for a minute costs one redraw from the page cache.

## Q2 Pod's list layout in menus

Reading settings, Chapters, Bookmarks and Dictionaries now use Q2 Pod's list
geometry (`reader/MenuLayout.hpp`): a 32 px bar with the title on the left and
the clock and battery on the right; eight 32 px rows; values right-aligned;
the selection full width (Classic's gloss band and chevron, Modern's accent,
Minimal's dot, inverted with no theme); a footer for the position and hints.
Painters and touch share one mapping, so a tap lands on the row under it, and
a tap on the bar or footer no longer picks a row by accident.

Differences from Q2 Pod kept on purpose:

- The clock sits on the right of the bar, not centred: Books' titles are long
  ("Reading settings"), and Q2 Pod also moves its clock aside for a title.
- The menu's clock is drawn when the menu opens and is not refreshed each
  minute while it stays open.
- The library keeps its 47 px cover rows (Q2 Pod's cover rows are 52 px).

  blending order, presenter with and without levels.
- `books_smooth_text_render`: the real reader's `--render-smooth` page has the
  same ink footprint as `--render`, with grey edges; `BOOKS_SMOOTH_TEXT=0`
  is plain black and white.

  charging, clamping, change detection, battery fill.

  paging away and back; footer redraws by itself when the battery changes and

  row taps on a scrolled list.
