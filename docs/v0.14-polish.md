# M6: polish for the Q2 (v0.14)

Status: **smooth text, status and list layout released in 1.2.0; TrueType text, corners and night mode on a branch, not released**. Checked
on the host (GCC, Clang, ASan) and on the fake Q2 framebuffer in all three
Q2 Pod themes and with no theme. Not yet measured on a physical Q2.

## Smooth text

Text is drawn with full 8-bit anti-aliasing in three ways.

1. **Book text from TrueType.** CrossPoint lays out each page (line
   breaks, word and glyph positions stay its own). Then, for each glyph a
   reading font's TrueType file has, the CrossPoint glyph is painted out and
   FreeType draws its full coverage at the same pen position and baseline
   (`reader/SmoothGlyphs.hpp`, through an opt-in hook in the renderer: off,
   nothing changes). The size is calibrated from the `.cpfont` itself: its
   advances (12.4 fixed point) across the alphabet against the TrueType
   advances, so it matches the converter's size without knowing its DPI. The
   release ships the nine reading families' source files unmodified in
   `.books/fonts/ttf/<slug>-<style>.ttf` (the OFL counts a subset as a
   modified font). The device build checks on the MIPS app that the TrueType
   glyphs land on CrossPoint's own (intersection over union of the ink).
2. **CrossPoint's 2-bit edges** for everything else: characters a TrueType
   file lacks (CJK, Indic and other scripts from their own `.cpfont`s), user
   fonts, and images. After a page is drawn in black and white, it is drawn
   twice more in the engine's own grayscale modes (`GRAYSCALE_LSB`,
   `GRAYSCALE_MSB`, as upstream does for e-ink), and the two planes give each
   ink pixel its level back (light grey, dark grey, black).
3. **Menus, bars and footers** in Q2 Pod's own TrueType faces
   (`reader/UiText.hpp`), see below.

The display keeps a coverage layer (`HalDisplay`): an 8-bit share of ink per
pixel and the ink or paper state it was drawn with. The coverage shows only
while the pixel still has that state, so anything drawn over it later (a
highlight, the progress bar) shows plain. Clearing the screen drops it; the
page cache keeps it. Blending is gamma-tuned (`Q2Display::blend`): coverage
is linear and the LCD sRGB, so edges are raised by 1.25 for dark text on a
light page and 1.6 for light text on a dark one, where thin strokes fade
most.

**Cost** (host, 20 page turns): 0.45 s of CPU against 0.35 s with 1.2.0's
2-bit smoothing; peak memory 1.8 MB higher (FreeType, glyph caches). Each new
page is drawn five times (black and white, two grey passes, erase, TrueType);
pages from the three-page cache cost nothing. **Page-turn time on a Q2 is not
measured yet.** `BOOKS_SMOOTH_GLYPHS=0` keeps the 2-bit edges;
`BOOKS_SMOOTH_TEXT=0` turns smoothing off.

## Menus in Q2 Pod's own faces, fixed in size

Menus, the bars and the footers draw with FreeType from the faces Q2 Pod
itself uses (`/release/assets/default/raw/fonts`), in its own order: the
theme's face (Classic: `classic-light.ttf`, Modern: `bold.ttf`, Minimal and
no theme: stock `default.ttf`), then `bold.ttf`, then `default.ttf` (which
has CJK), then a language pack's font (`/mnt/data/q2-font.ttf`, e.g. Thai).
Sizes are Q2 Pod's: list rows 27 px in Classic and 24 px in the others,
bars 21 px. Changing the reading font or size no longer touches the menus.
Without the faces (older firmware) the menus fall back to the fixed
built-in font, never the reading font. CJK and Thai menu letters come with
the system faces; Books' translation tables for those languages are still
to be written.

The library, the menus and dialogs show **no control hints** (Wheel, Centre,
OK, Back): the Q2's keys work as everywhere. A footer appears only for a
notice; the online details screen keeps "Download" as its tap target.

## Text clear of the rounded corners

The Q2's glass rounds its corners. Books uses Q2 Pod's calibration
(`tools/ipod.py` `CORNER_R`, 80 px, fitted to device photos, plus 4 px of
slack; `MenuLayout::cornerX`):

- Page text runs from y 40 to 282, where a 14 px margin clears the glass.
- The top band (y 0 to 40) holds the book title on the left, the clock in the
  middle and the battery on the right; the bottom band holds the progress
  bar and the page. Both are inset by the corner at their rows.
- Menu bars, menu notices and the library's footer and title are inset the
  same way. `BOOKS_CORNER_CHECK=1` reports any ink under the glass per frame;
  every screen checked reports none in the themes (the inverted bars of the
  no-theme look are backgrounds).

## Night mode follows the system

Night mode means a dark page, whatever the theme. With nothing chosen it
follows Q2 Pod: a dark theme (Modern, Minimal, Classic with Light Mode off)
reads in night mode, a light one does not. Turning it to the other look is
saved; turning it back to the theme's look follows the theme again
(`reading-settings.conf` v4: 0 follow, 1 off, 2 on; older files read their
old "off" as follow). It swaps the page's ink and paper instead of inverting
the page, so smooth text and highlights stay as drawn; menus keep the
theme's colours.

## Checks

- `books_ui_text_truetype`: Q2 Pod's px sizing, measuring, face order and
  fallback, real edge coverage, clipping, cleared screens.
- `books_smooth_glyphs_truetype`: with a TrueType file for the reading font
  (Liberation Sans standing in, `BOOKS_TEST_FONTS`), calibrated size,
  agreement with CrossPoint's glyphs, full coverage; off gives 2-bit edges.
- `books_smooth_text_levels`, `books_smooth_text_render`: the coverage
  layer, plane decoding, blending.
- `books_reader_state_and_bookmarks`: night mode follow/off/on and the v4
  settings file.
- Device build: `--smooth-check` on the MIPS app with the real Noto files.
- Full suites: GCC, Clang and ASan/UBSan 95/95, with and without the card
  fonts; card regression on the fake Q2.
