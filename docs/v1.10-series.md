# v1.10 M1 — Series metadata

Reuse the bounded OPF parser (eight collection candidates). Support Calibre series and series_index attributes and EPUB3 belongs-to-collection with local collection-type=series and group-position refinements, including refinements preceding the declaration. Calibre title takes precedence; a missing Calibre volume may be filled from an exact matching EPUB3 series. Normalize Calibre whitespace. Accept nonnegative decimal positions up to one million, including zero and fractions, independently of locale. Reject malformed, nonfinite, exponent and hexadecimal positions; retain the series without a volume. Existing numeric series ordering remains in the shelf; M2 will refine normalized cache identity and duplicate handling. Parser regression assertions added to existing OPF test; not yet executed. No dependency or cache format changes in M1.
