CrossPod

A small reader for Shanling Q2 Pod.

Turn the wheel to read the next page. Use Previous to go backwards, Center for the next page, and Back to exit.

This is a prototype of a future CrossPoint-inspired EPUB reader for Q2 Pod. EPUB files are not supported yet.

Replace /.crosspod/book.txt on your microSD with your own plain text file.

The first release focuses on stability and readable text. Future releases will add a library, Unicode, reading positions, and EPUB support.
